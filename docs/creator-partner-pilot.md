# Creator partner pilot

This is a draft outreach and onboarding pack. No messages should be sent until the founder approves the recipient list and final copy.

## Pilot offer

Invite 5–10 trusted food creators to publish one small, themed recipe book of 8–15 recipes. Kitchen Companion turns the book into an interactive planning object: followers can save it to their shelf, schedule meals, add missing ingredients to shopping, follow linked video guidance and optionally log nutrition.

The pilot is editorial, not a marketplace launch. There are no payments or exclusivity commitments in beta. Creators retain attribution and approve every published recipe, image and media link.

## Good early profiles

- credible specialists with a recognisable point of view;
- recipe-led audiences rather than general lifestyle audiences;
- strong existing short-form or YouTube cooking demonstrations;
- clear allergen/dietary communication;
- willingness to test a themed collection with a small cohort.

Examples such as Doctor Vegan should be researched and contacted only after verifying current identity, business contact route and fit. Do not scrape private contact details.

## Outreach controls

- No outreach is sent until the founder approves the named recipient and exact final copy.
- Record the public source URL, date checked, channel used, consent and response status.
- Do not import, paraphrase or publish a creator's recipe without written permission and provenance.
- Do not promise revenue share, exclusivity, audience size or a launch date during the editorial beta.
- Use a short discovery call first; only request recipe assets after the creator agrees to the pilot shape.

## Onboarding checklist

- creator name, public bio, avatar and verified links;
- book title, promise, audience and edition number;
- recipe source/provenance confirmation;
- ingredients in structured units, timings, servings and allergens;
- step-by-step instructions and optional YouTube/audio links;
- image rights and attribution;
- creator approval and editorial approval recorded before publication;
- version notes for every post-publication change.
